Privacy Policy

AYG Foods — Employee Onboarding Feedback SMS Program

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

AYG Foods ("we," "us," or "our") operates an SMS-based onboarding feedback messaging program ("Program") to communicate with current employees approximately 30 days after their start date, to collect onboarding feedback and, where an employee could not be reached by phone, to request a callback. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the personal information you provide as a participant in this Program.

2. Information We Collect

We collect your name, mobile phone number, employment/start-date information, and the signed consent record you provide during onboarding. Your mobile phone number is collected solely for the purpose of sending you Program-related SMS messages, such as callback requests following an unanswered onboarding feedback call.

3. No Sharing of Mobile Numbers

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose your mobile phone number to any third party for their marketing purposes. Your mobile number will never be shared with outside parties for any purpose other than facilitating the delivery of SMS messages as part of AYG Foods' employee onboarding feedback process.

4. Message Frequency

Message frequency is limited and tied to a single onboarding milestone. Eligible employees typically receive a small number of messages around their 30-day onboarding check-in (for example, a callback request if a scheduled call is missed, and at most one follow-up reminder). We will only contact you when there is relevant activity related to your onboarding check-in.

5. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Standard carrier message and data rates may be charged by your mobile service provider for SMS messages sent and received. Please check with your carrier if you are unsure about your plan's messaging rates.

6. How to Opt Out

You may opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. After opting out, you will receive a single confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. To re-enroll, reply START.

7. Help

For help, reply HELP to any message or contact us at hr@aygfoods.com.

8. Consent Basis

Participation in this Program is based on a written "SMS Communication Consent" form signed by the employee with their manager during onboarding. Consent is voluntary and is not a condition of continued employment.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. Your data is stored securely and access is limited to authorized AYG Foods personnel.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
