Privacy Policy

AYG Foods HR Platform — SMS Messaging Program

Effective Date: June 18, 2026

1. Introduction

AYG Foods ("we," "us," or "our") operates an SMS-based recruitment messaging program to communicate with job applicants and candidates regarding scheduling, interviews, and employment opportunities. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the personal information you provide when participating in this program.

2. Information We Collect

We collect your name, phone number, and information you provide during the application process. Your mobile phone number is collected solely for the purpose of sending you recruitment-related SMS messages.

3. No Sharing of Mobile Numbers

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose your mobile phone number to any third party for their marketing purposes. Your mobile number will never be shared with outside parties for any purpose other than facilitating the delivery of SMS messages as part of AYG Foods' recruitment process.

4. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your application status and scheduled interview appointments. You may receive up to 5 messages per month related to interview scheduling, appointment reminders, and recruitment updates. We will only contact you when there is relevant activity related to your application.

5. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Standard carrier message and data rates may be charged by your mobile service provider for SMS messages sent and received. Please check with your carrier if you are unsure about your plan's messaging rates.

6. How to Opt Out

You may opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. After opting out, you will receive a single confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. To re-enroll, reply START.

7. Help

For help, reply HELP to any message or contact us at hr@aygfoods.com.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. Your data is stored securely and access is limited to authorized AYG Foods personnel.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
