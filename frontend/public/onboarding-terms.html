AYG Foods — Employee Onboarding Feedback SMS Program

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

1. Program Description

AYG Foods operates an SMS employee onboarding feedback program ("Program") to contact current employees approximately 30 days after their start date to collect onboarding feedback. If an employee does not answer the scheduled call, AYG Foods sends a single SMS message asking them to call back.

2. Consent to Receive Messages

Only current employees who signed a written "SMS Communication Consent" form with their manager during onboarding, agreeing to receive work-related SMS from AYG Foods, are enrolled in this Program. Consent is voluntary and is not a condition of continued employment.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency is limited to your 30-day onboarding check-in. You may receive up to 2 messages related to a missed onboarding feedback call and a single follow-up reminder. We will only send messages relevant to your onboarding check-in.

4. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. You are responsible for any charges your mobile carrier applies for sending or receiving text messages. Contact your carrier if you are unsure about applicable rates.

5. How to Opt Out

You may opt out of the Program at any time by replying STOP to any message received from AYG Foods. Upon receipt of your STOP request, we will send a final confirmation message and you will no longer receive SMS messages from us. To re-enroll in the Program, reply START.

6. Help

For assistance, reply HELP to any message, or contact us directly at hr@aygfoods.com.

7. Eligible Participants

This Program is available only to current AYG Foods employees who signed the written SMS Communication Consent form during onboarding and provided a valid U.S. mobile phone number. By participating, you confirm that you are the owner or authorized user of the mobile number provided.

8. Supported Carriers

AYG Foods' SMS Program is supported by major U.S. carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, and others. Carrier participation may change without notice. AYG Foods and its messaging partners are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

9. Privacy

Your mobile number will not be shared with third parties for marketing purposes. For full details on how we handle your data, please review our Privacy Policy.

10. Limitation of Liability

AYG Foods is not responsible for any delays or failures in the receipt of any SMS messages as delivery is subject to effective transmission by your mobile carrier. AYG Foods reserves the right to alter or discontinue the Program at any time.
